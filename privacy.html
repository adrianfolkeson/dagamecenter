Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-07 · dagamecenter.com

1. What we collect

DA Game Center collects minimal data to operate:

  • Game progress — saved locally in your browser (localStorage). Never sent to our servers.
  • Ad data — Google AdSense may collect device identifiers to show relevant ads. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Analytics — anonymous usage stats (page views, session length) via privacy-friendly analytics.

2. How we use it

Your game data stays on your device. We never sell, share or transfer personal data to third parties except the services listed below.

3. Third parties

  • Google AdSense — advertising. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Cookies

We use localStorage for game saves. Google AdSense uses cookies for ad personalization. You can opt out via your browser settings or Google's ad settings.

5. Children

Our games are rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Contact

Questions: privacy@dagamecenter.com
